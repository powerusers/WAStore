The problem

WhatsApp orders work — until the list gets long.

Your customers already message you on WhatsApp. But typing "2kg atta, 1 dal, 3 Maggi…" back and forth is slow for them and painful for you.

Without a catalog

Long text lists, voice notes, missed items, price confusion, and no record of what was ordered until you scroll the chat.

With Order on WhatsApp

Share one link. They browse, search, and cart. Checkout opens WhatsApp with a formatted order — name, phone, address, and line items included.

How it works

Three steps. Familiar channel.

Built for how Indian local stores actually sell — not for replacing WhatsApp, but for making it smoother.

1

Share your store link

Put it on WhatsApp status, print a QR code, or send it in your broadcast list. Each store gets its own URL and branding.

2

Customer browses & carts

Mobile catalog with search, categories, and cart. Works in English or Hindi. They can install it to their home screen like an app.

3

Order lands in WhatsApp

One tap opens WhatsApp with the full order pre-filled. You confirm, deliver, and update status — customer can track with order ID + phone.
